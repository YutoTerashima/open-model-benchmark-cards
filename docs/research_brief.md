# Research Brief

## Problem

Benchmark results are often hard to audit because metrics, reproduction commands, and
limitations are scattered. Model cards need a lightweight, repeatable benchmark section.

## Method

The generator converts structured JSON into Markdown cards with task, metrics,
reproduction command, and limitations.

## Depth Signal

The project emphasizes reproducibility and honest reporting over leaderboard theater.
